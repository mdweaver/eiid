\section{Conventions and cited results}\label{sec:conv}

\subsection{Notation}

The notation is that of the main text. Units are $i=1,\dots,n$; rows (groups)
$r,s=1,\dots,R$; columns (outcomes) $c=1,\dots,C$. $X\in\Delta^{R-1}$ and
$T\in\Delta^{C-1}$ are the row and column shares, $N$ the unit size, $D$ the
district when there are districts, and $V$ the design variables ($V=(X,N)$ in the
pooled model, $V=(X,N,D)$ with districts). $\Bset=(\Delta^{C-1})^R$ is the set of
rate matrices, with inner product $\langle u,b\rangle=\sum_{rc}u_{rc}b_{rc}$ and
Frobenius norm; every $b\in\Bset$ has $\|b\|\le\sqrt R$. $J=\one\one^\top$.
$S(x,t)=\{b\in\Bset:b^\top x=t\}$ is the unit Duncan--Davis polytope and
$h_S(u;x,t)=\max_{b\in S(x,t)}\langle u,b\rangle$ its support function.
$H=(H_1,\dots,H_C)$, $M_H(x)_{rc}=e_r^\top H_cx$, $Q_c=\sym H_c$, $K_c=\skw H_c$,
$\Kspace=\{K:K_c^\top=-K_c,\sum_cK_c=0\}$ and $k$ the free coordinates $k_{c,rs}$
($c<C$, $r<s$). The model $\mathcal M(H;A)$ is defined by (S1)--(S3) of the main
text, $\psi(u;v)=\int h_S(u;x,t)P_{T\mid V=v}(dt)$, and $\mu_r=\E[NX_r]>0$,
$\mu_{rs}=\E[NX_rX_s]$, with $\E[N]<\infty$ throughout (standing integrability).
For a probability measure $P_X$ and a closed set $A$, $\supp_AP_X$ is the support of
the restriction $E\mapsto P(X\in A\cap E)$. It equals $\supp P_X$ when
$P(X\in A)=1$, and in general can be strictly smaller than $\supp P_X\cap A$.

The following elementary fact is used repeatedly.

\begin{lemma}[Agreement on the restricted support]\label{lem:supp}
If continuous $f,g$ agree $P_X$-almost everywhere on $A$, they agree on $\supp_AP_X$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $f(x_0)\ne g(x_0)$ at some $x_0\in\supp_AP_X$, then $f\ne g$ on an open set
$U\ni x_0$, and $P(X\in A\cap U)>0$ by the definition of the support, a
contradiction.
\end{proof}

Gale's theorem & A transportation problem is feasible iff every set of supply nodes demands no more than the capacity of its neighbours \citep[Theorem 1]{gale1957}. \\

CLR & Intersection-bound inference with parametric bounding functions over a finite index set, under Condition~P \citep[Theorem 4, with Lemma 4 and Example 1]{clr2013}. \\
Uniform CLT & The multivariate CLT holds uniformly over convex sets \citep[Theorem 17.5]{bhattacharyarao1976}. \\
Lindeberg CLT & For independent summands \citep[Section 27]{billingsley1995}. \\
Hoeffding & For independent $Y_i\in[a_i,b_i]$, $P(\sum_i(Y_i-\E Y_i)\ge t)\le\exp\{-2t^2/\sum_i(b_i-a_i)^2\}$ \citep[Theorem 2]{hoeffding1963}. \\
